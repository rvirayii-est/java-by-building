# Code of Conduct

Java by Building is for beginners, and many of them are learning in a second or third language, on a modest computer, after work or
between classes. This project should feel safe and welcoming for all of them, and for the teachers and contributors who help.

## What we expect
- **Be kind and patient.** Every expert was once stuck on a missing semicolon. Answer the question that was asked, in plain words.
- **Be respectful** of different backgrounds, languages, ages, genders, abilities, beliefs and levels of experience.
- **Give and accept feedback gracefully.** Say what is good and what to change; critique code and ideas, never people.
- **Do not condescend.** "It's easy" and "just do X" help nobody. Explain why.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal data, passwords, or screenshots with private information. If you leak a secret yourself, say so, change it, and ask for help cleaning up.
- **Keep it on topic and safe for learners.** No harassment, discrimination, sexual content, threats, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions, code comments or commit messages.

## What is not acceptable
Harassment or condescension of any kind, insults, unwelcome attention, publishing someone's private information, deliberately sabotaging someone's work, and encouraging
any of these. Repeated or serious breaches lead to removal of the comment, a warning, or a ban from the project.

## Where it applies
In every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public (for example teaching it in a class or a forum).

## Reporting
If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, tell a maintainer privately, by opening an issue that asks for a private contact (do not put the details in it)
or by contacting the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with care and kept confidential as far as possible.
Maintainers who are involved in a report will step back from deciding on it.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may warn or ban people. They will explain their decision.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, shortened for readers who are new to open source.
